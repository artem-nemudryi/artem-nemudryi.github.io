Godfred is originally from Ghana, where he earned his B.Sc. in Biomedical Sciences from the University of Cape Coast. He later moved to France, where he earned his Master’s in Infectious Diseases and Ph.D. in Microbiology from Université Paris-Saclay. He is currently a Postdoctoral Associate at the University of Florida. His research interests focus on host–pathogen interactions and the molecular mechanisms that shape immunity across biological systems. Outside of the lab, Godfred enjoys sports, music, movies, exploring new places, and discovering different cultures.
